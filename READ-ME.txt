# Support

## WebCup 2026

**Organized by Mont Lubin College**

Welcome to the WebCup Support Centre. Our team is available throughout the competition to assist participants with any competition-related issues.

---

## Technical Support

If you experience any technical problems, such as:

* The competition website is not loading.
* A page or feature is not working correctly.
* Your project files cannot be accessed.
* Your assigned computer encounters an error.
* The timer or submission system is not functioning.

Please **raise your hand immediately** and wait for a competition official. Do not attempt to fix the issue yourself unless instructed by an organizer.

---

## Competition Assistance

If you have questions regarding:

* Competition rules
* Challenge requirements
* Submission procedures
* Competition schedule
* Judging criteria

Please speak to a WebCup official or a member of the organizing committee.

---

## Reporting Issues

To ensure a fair competition:

* Report technical issues as soon as they occur.
* Do not attempt to access another participant's computer.
* Do not modify competition software or system files.
* Follow the instructions given by competition supervisors.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Can I use the internet?

No. WebCup is an **offline competition**, and internet access is not permitted unless specifically authorized by the organizers.

### What should I do if my computer freezes?

Raise your hand immediately and wait for a competition official. Do not restart the computer unless instructed.

### Can I use my own USB drive?

Only if permission has been granted by the organizers before the competition begins.

### Can I ask judges for programming help?

No. Judges may clarify the competition rules but will not provide coding assistance or solutions.

### What happens if I accidentally close the website?

Notify a competition official immediately. They will assist you in reopening the competition system if appropriate.
