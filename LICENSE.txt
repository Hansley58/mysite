# WebCup 2026 – Terms and Conditions

**Organized by Mont Lubin College**

**Last Updated:** July 2026

## 1. Acceptance of Terms

By accessing or using the WebCup offline competition website, all participants acknowledge that they have read, understood, and agreed to these Terms and Conditions.

## 2. Eligibility

* WebCup is open only to officially registered participants.
* Participants must comply with all competition rules and instructions provided by the organizers.
* Any participant found violating these Terms and Conditions may be disqualified.

## 3. Offline Competition

* WebCup is conducted using an offline website and does not require an internet connection.
* Participants are not permitted to access the internet or any external online resources unless expressly authorized by the organizers.
* Any attempt to bypass this restriction may result in immediate disqualification.

## 4. Fair Play

Participants must:

* Submit original work.
* Respect other participants and competition officials.
* Follow the competition schedule and instructions.
* Use only the software and resources permitted by the organizers.

## 5. Prohibited Activities

Participants must not:

* Copy or plagiarize another participant's work.
* Use unauthorized software, AI tools, external storage devices, or communication devices unless permitted.
* Attempt to modify or interfere with the competition website or computer system.
* Access restricted files or folders.
* Disrupt the competition or interfere with another participant's work.

## 6. Intellectual Property

All submitted projects remain the intellectual property of their creators. However, by participating in WebCup, participants grant Mont Lubin College permission to evaluate, showcase, archive, and promote their submissions for educational and promotional purposes.

## 7. Data Protection

* Personal information collected during registration will be used solely for competition administration.
* As the competition website operates offline, participant information is not transmitted over the internet.
* Organizers will take reasonable measures to protect participant information.

## 8. Technical Issues

* Participants should report any technical issue immediately to a competition supervisor.
* Only authorized officials may assist with technical problems.
* The organizers are not responsible for work lost due to participant error or unauthorized modifications.

## 9. Judging

All submissions will be evaluated according to the official WebCup judging criteria. The judges' decisions are final and cannot be appealed.

## 10. Disqualification

Participants may be disqualified for:

* Cheating or plagiarism.
* Violating competition rules.
* Using unauthorized materials or devices.
* Unsportsmanlike conduct.
* Damaging competition equipment or software.

## 11. Liability

Mont Lubin College, the WebCup organizing committee, and competition officials shall not be held liable for any damage, loss of files, or technical issues beyond their reasonable control.

## 12. Changes to These Terms

The organizers reserve the right to update these Terms and Conditions before the start of the competition. Participants will be informed of any significant changes.
